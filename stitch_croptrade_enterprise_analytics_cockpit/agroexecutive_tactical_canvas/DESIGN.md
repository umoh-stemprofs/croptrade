## Brand & Style

The design system projects mission-critical authority, fiscal rigor, and agricultural intelligence. Designed specifically for institutional grain traders, corporate agronomists, and commodity portfolio directors, the visual tone blends high-density cockpit precision with institutional-grade stability. 

Borrowing from modern corporate-minimalism with high-contrast tactical cues, the interface removes extraneous ornamentation in favor of scannable metric density, decisive boundaries, and immediate status recognition. The visual weight communicates sovereign trust, real-time liquidity, and yield predictability across global harvest logistics.

## Colors

The palette operates across dual dynamic modes engineered for divergent operational environments: high-ambient trading desks (Light Mode) and low-light operations terminals (Dark Mode).

### Light Mode (Default)
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Off-white canvas)
- **Surface Layer:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white card surfaces)
- **Border Structural:** `#E2E8F0` (Crisp perimeter definition)
- **Brand Primary:** `#008751` (Emerald Green — represents yield authority, positive valuation, agricultural yield)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#0284C7` (Deep Ocean Blue — metric streams, futures pricing, maritime logistics)
- **Alert / Warning:** `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber — yield hazard, market volatility, weather anomaly)
- **Primary Text:** `#0F172A` (Dark Slate — high-contrast clarity for dense figures)
- **Secondary Text:** `#64748B` (Muted Slate — non-essential metric headers and subtext)

### Dark Mode
- **Canvas Base:** `#0F172A` (Deep Obsidian foundation)
- **Surface Layer:** `#1E293B` (Dark Slate Card elevation)
- **Border Structural:** `#334155` (Slate boundary contrast)
- **Brand Primary:** `#10B981` (Bright Emerald — luminescent signal status)
- **Secondary Accent:** `#06B6D4` (Electric Cyan — high-definition telemetry)
- **Alert / Warning:** `#FBBF24` (Bright Gold — high-visibility incident notification)
- **Primary Text:** `#F8FAFC` (Crisp White — high-contrast data legibility)
- **Secondary Text:** `#94A3B8` (Cool Gray — subdued tabular metadata)

### Application Rules
- Apply primary emerald exclusively to positive yield performance, operational confirmation states, and key transactional triggers.
- Reserve electric cyan and deep ocean blue for synthetic trade paths, water table indices, and analytical projections.
- Always maintain a strict 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio for tabular numbers against container surfaces.

## Typography

The typography pairs geometric authority with clinical data performance:
- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for structural geometry, executive posture, and premium weight distribution. Tighter letter tracking reduces sprawling horizontal space on dashboards.
- **Body & Data Displays (Inter):** Applied across core data surfaces, tabular listings, analytical legends, and forms. Numeric tabular features (`tnum`, `zero`) must be toggled for commodity price tickers, grain metric weights, and volume tables to ensure precise vertical digit stacking.
- **Uppercase Data Labels:** Small labels (`label-sm`) require uppercase rendering with slight tracking expansion (`0.04em`) to establish visual separation when parked above metric values.

## Layout & Spacing

The system enforces a high-density, multi-panel fluid grid calibrated for complex monitor setups and field-ready tablets.

### Grid Rhythm & Columns
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` page margin, `1.5rem` gutter. Enables standard 3-metric, 4-metric, or 8/4 split layouts (Trading Chart / Order Book).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` margins, `1rem` gutter. Sidebars collapse to icon rails; metric widgets stack into 2x2 grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` margin, `0.75rem` gutter. Dense vertical stack with horizontal scrolling charts and metric cards.

### Spacing Usage
- `space-xs` (4px): Metric deltas, badge internal padding, micro icon spacing.
- `space-sm` (8px): Form input padding (vertical), gap between metric labels and numeric outputs.
- `space-md` (16px): Card internal padding, vertical stacking between card modules.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-section spacing within an active dashboard container.
- `space-xl` (32px): Primary separation between major analytical sections (e.g., Regional Yield Overview vs. Futures Pipeline).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on structural containment and low-key ambient boundaries over heavy drop-shadows.

### Light Mode Elevation
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#F8FAFC`.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Toolbars):** Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px solid `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px 0px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Popovers, Dropdowns, Date Pickers):** Background `#FFFFFF`, bordered by 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals & Hazard Overlays):** Full backdrop scrim `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.60)` with surface `#FFFFFF` elevated with `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

### Dark Mode Elevation
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#0F172A`.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards):** Background `#1E293B`, framed with 1px solid `#334155`. Zero drop shadow; contrast is purely structural.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Panels & Flyouts):** Background `#1E293B` with 1px solid `#475569`. Diffuse shadow: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals):** Backdrop scrim `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)` with card `#1E293B` and active stroke `#475569`.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 1 (Soft)** shape geometry:
- Standard elements (buttons, text inputs, small badges): `0.25rem` (4px).
- Container cards, analytical modular blocks, and split panes: `0.5rem` (8px).
- Modals, large slide-out drawers, and macro overlay envelopes: `0.75rem` (12px).

This tight corner geometry maximizes screen real estate, maintains sharp alignment with data tables, and projects an austere, professional demeanor suitable for commodity-grade financial workflows. Pill-shaped radii are banned except for status indicator dots.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in Brand Primary (`#008751` Light / `#10B981` Dark), text is high-contrast white. Height: 36px (compact density). Border-radius: 4px. Subtle hover darkening in light mode (+8% black) or brightness boost in dark mode.
- **Secondary (Data Actions):** Ghost card styling. Surface is transparent or card-matched, enclosed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0` Light / `#334155` Dark). Text is Primary Text.
- **Destructive/Hazard:** 1px border colored via alert tones or flat dark crimson with high-contrast text.

### Data Cards & KPI Tiles
- Built with a 1px rigid outer border, strictly padded at `space-md` (16px).
- Layout: Header holds `label-sm` in Secondary Text alongside an optional sparkline or trend badge. Center hosts bold numeric readouts using `headline-md` or `headline-lg` with tabular numbers enabled. Footer holds contextual comparative delta (e.g., `+4.2% vs. 5-yr avg`).

### Input Fields & Select Controls
- Height: 36px. Border: 1px solid (`#E2E8F0` Light / `#334155` Dark).
- Background: Surface white in Light Mode; `#0F172A` inset well in Dark Mode.
- Focus State: Replaces neutral border with a crisp 1px ring in Secondary Accent (`#0284C7` Light / `#06B6D4` Dark) with zero ambient blur ring.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 16px × 16px square with 2px corner radius for checkboxes; 16px circular for radios.
- Unselected: Inset surface with 1.5px border (`#94A3B8`).
- Selected: Filled with Brand Primary with white check/dot glyph centered.

### Metric Chips & Status Badges
- Height: 22px. Padding: 0 8px. Text style: `label-sm`.
- **Surplus/Bullish:** Emerald background tint at 10% opacity with solid emerald text.
- **Deficit/Bearish:** Crimson/Amber background tint at 10% opacity with solid warning text.
- **Neutral/Hedged:** Slate tint at 10% opacity with secondary text.

### Agritech-Specific Component: Telemetry Stream Bar
- High-density status strip positioned at card headers or screen baselines displaying real-time moisture index, elevator storage utilization, and rail car availability. Formatted using inline monospace text, subtle dividing pipes (`#E2E8F0` / `#334155`), and micro indicator pips (6px green/amber pulses).
